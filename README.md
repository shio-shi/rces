# Rblox Hub

Generate a website similar to the 2021 roblox website, first there will be the sign up and log in page where you will be able to sign up by entering a username and a password, after you sign up you will be redirected to the home page, on the left side bar there will be buttons such as: Home, Profile, Friends, Inventory, Trade and Promocodes, the home button will redirect you to the home page, profile will redirect to your profile, friends will redirect you to the friends page that will show your pending friend requests, your friends, your followers and the people you follow, inventory will redirect to your inventory with all your items, trade will redirect you to the trades page where you will see the trades you got sent, the trades you sent, trades you completed and trades that are inactive, promocode will redirect to the promocodes page where you will be able to enter promocodes, on the top bar there will be a button called catalog which will redirect to the catalog page with all items that were uploaded, on the top bar there will also be a search bar where you will be able to search up users by their username and view other people's profiles, send them a friend request, send a trade and view their inventory, in the top right will be rawribux, everyone will automatically get 100 rawribux every 24 hours and 100 starter rawribux after signing up on the website, next to rawribux there will be the settings button which will redirect to the settings page where you will be able to edit your profile such as changing your username which will cost 1000 rawribux to do, change your password, enter a description that will be visible on your profile and log out of your account, now for the admin panel: only i will be able to access the admin panel with a password that only i know, in the admin panel i will be able to publish items, there will be types of items: normal on sale items, Limited items and Limited U items, there will also be types of items such as: hat, hair, face, neck, shoulder, front, back, waist, gear and faces, i will be able to add those types to items, description and image to them, when i publish a limited or a limited U item i will be able to set a timer, while the timer is still running the item can be bought by anyone for the original price and after the timer is out the item goes limited, after which the owners of the item can re sell it and others can buy it, all items and their stats will be visible on the catalog page, you will be able to click each item and it will open in a new page, showing it's name, type, if it's limited or limited U, description, price, it's owners and resellers, how many copies were sold before the item went limited and it's recent average price, the recent average price will change after someone buys an item after it's already limited, if nobody is selling an item then instead of it's price it will say "No one is currently selling this item.", in the admin panel i will also be able to ban and unban players, if you go to a banned player's profile it will say "Page cannot be found or no longer exists", i will also be able to write a reason to why the player was banned and the banned player will not be able to access the website and instead will only be able to see the ban message which will say for how long they are banned and the reason, i will be able to give 1 day, 3 day, 7 days, 14 days, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year and permanent bans, i will also be able to give users robux, keep everything as close as possible to the 2021 roblox website
